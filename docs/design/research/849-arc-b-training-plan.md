# #849 Arc B — the conditioning plan

Seed material for Arc B of [`849-lattice-scenario-arcs.md`](849-lattice-scenario-arcs.md). This is
the plan the runner *intended* to follow; the arc's timeline records what actually happened
against it. A conservative 17-week return-to-running plan, June 15 → October 15, targeting a
sub-10:00/mi 5K. Rebuild durability first, then add controlled speed.

**Assumptions.** Lifting continues 3×/week. Brisk walking 45–60 min is comfortable. Goal race
pace ≈ 9:50–9:55/mi. Easy running is conversational, ~11:30–13:00/mi or slower.

**Weekly layout.** Mon strength · **Tue easy run or run/walk** · Wed strength · **Thu quality
run** · Fri rest/mobility · **Sat longer easy run** · Sun strength, easy walk, or rest.
Three runs/week at first, optionally four later. No maximal leg work the day before a quality run.

## Phase 1 — Rebuild the running habit (Jun 15 – Jul 12)
Goal: legs, tendons, calves, feet and hips used to impact again. No speedwork. Finish every
session feeling like you could do more.

| wk | Mon | Tue | Thu | Sat |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | 06-15 | run/walk 25 min, 1 jog / 2 walk | run/walk 25 min, 1/2 | brisk walk 40–50 min |
| 2 | 06-22 | run/walk 28 min, 2/2 | run/walk 28 min, 2/2 | run/walk 30 min, 2/2 |
| 3 | 06-29 | run/walk 30 min, 3/2 | run/walk 30 min, 4/2 | easy run/walk 35 min |
| 4 | 07-06 | easy continuous 20 min if comfortable | run/walk 30 min | easy run/walk 40 min |

## Phase 2 — Build continuous running (Jul 13 – Aug 9)
Goal: comfortable continuous running and an established weekly long run. Strides are smooth,
quick, relaxed — not sprints.

| wk | Mon | Tue | Thu | Sat |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 5 | 07-13 | easy 25 min | easy 25 min + 4 × 15-sec strides | long easy run/walk 40 min |
| 6 | 07-20 | easy 30 min | easy 25 min + 5 strides | long easy 45 min |
| 7 | 07-27 | easy 30 min | **progression:** 20 min easy, then 5 min a little quicker | long easy 50 min |
| 8 | 08-03 | *cutback* easy 25 min | easy 25 min + 4 strides | long easy 40 min |

## Phase 3 — Add 5K-specific fitness (Aug 10 – Sep 6)
Goal: controlled faster running without overloading. "Comfortably hard" ≈ 10K effort, not all-out.

| wk | Mon | Tue | Thu | Sat |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 9 | 08-10 | easy 35 min | 6 × 1 min at ~9:45–10:00/mi, 2 min easy | long easy 55 min |
| 10 | 08-17 | easy 35 min + 4 strides | 4 × 3 min at ~10:00/mi, 2 min easy | long easy 60 min |
| 11 | 08-24 | easy 35–40 min | tempo 3 × 6 min comfortably hard, 3 min easy | long easy 60 min |
| 12 | 08-31 | *cutback* easy 30 min | 8 × 1 min brisk, 90 sec easy | long easy 45–50 min |

## Phase 4 — Sharpen toward sub-10 pace (Sep 7 – Oct 4)
Goal: make goal pace feel familiar and sustainable.

| wk | Mon | Tue | Thu | Sat |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 13 | 09-07 | easy 40 min | 5 × 3 min at 9:45–9:55/mi, 2 min easy | long easy 60–65 min |
| 14 | 09-14 | easy 35 min + 5 strides | 3 × 8 min at 10:00/mi, 3 min easy | long easy 55–60 min |
| 15 | 09-21 | easy 40 min | 6 × 2 min slightly faster than goal pace, 2 min easy | long easy 50–55 min |
| 16 | 09-28 | *rehearsal* easy 30–35 min | **2 × 1 mile at 9:50–10:00/mi, 4 min easy between** | long easy 45–50 min |

**If the 2 × 1 mile workout feels controlled, you are in range.**

## Taper and race (Oct 5 – Oct 15)

| wk | Mon | sessions |
|---|---|---|
| 17 | 10-05 | easy 30 min + 4 strides · 6 × 1 min at goal pace, 2 min easy · easy 20–25 min |
| race | 10-12 | 3–4 days out: 20 min easy + 4 short strides · 1–2 days out: rest or 15–20 min walk · **Thu 10-15 race** |

Race pacing: mile 1 10:05–10:10 · mile 2 9:55–10:00 · mile 3 9:40–9:50 · final 0.1 push. Let the
first 8–10 minutes feel almost too easy; the biggest mistake is proving fitness in mile 1.

## Strength adjustments (from mid-August)
No heavy squats/deadlifts within 24 h before a quality run. One heavy lower-body day, one
moderate full-body day, one lighter power/mobility day. Add calf raises, tibialis raises,
single-leg balance and hip abduction 2–3×/week.

## Red flags — back off for
Achilles pain · sharp knee pain · foot pain that changes the stride · calf tightness that worsens
as you run · soreness lasting more than 48 h after an easy run. The limiter is tissue tolerance
more than cardiovascular fitness: arrive in October healthy and consistent and sub-10 is realistic.
